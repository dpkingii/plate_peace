## Detailed description

QuietPlate hides calorie numbers on restaurant and menu websites, so you can browse and order without the numbers grabbing your attention.

Menu sites increasingly print a calorie count next to every single item. For a lot of people, that's not helpful information — it's just noise that gets in the way of enjoying a meal out. QuietPlate quietly tucks those numbers away.

How it works:
- Turn it on with one click from the toolbar icon
- QuietPlate scans the page you're on and hides calorie numbers as you browse
- Works automatically on menu sites as you navigate
- Light and dark themes to match your browser
- See a number that slipped through, or a page where nothing happened? Flag it with the report icon and QuietPlate's developer will take a look

Privacy:
- All scanning happens locally in your browser — page content is never sent anywhere
- QuietPlate only stores your on/off and theme preferences
- The only thing ever sent off your device is the page URL, and only if you deliberately click "report this page"
- Full privacy policy: https://github.com/dpkingii/quietplate/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

QuietPlate is free, open source, and doesn't run ads or track you.
